# ADR 0001: Community-moderated contributions

Status: Accepted

## Context
Lexicon RT depends on community knowledge but must resist nonsense, attacks, accidental misinformation, and silent rewriting of published language knowledge.

## Decision
Treat every community addition or edit as a contribution that produces a candidate revision. Candidate revisions require moderation before publication. The number of approvals is configurable. Published revisions and provenance are retained.

## Consequences
The persisted model needs contributions, recoverable revisions and moderation records rather than a single editable word table. The UI can remain simple while the audit trail remains rich.
