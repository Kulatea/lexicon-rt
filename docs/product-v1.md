# Lexicon RT v1 product direction

Lexicon RT is a community-built Rotuman dictionary designed to become a community-governed language resource.

## V1
- One search across Rotuman words and English meanings.
- Simple contribution flow: Rotuman word + English meaning required; richer detail optional.
- Contributions are proposals, not published dictionary truth.
- Trusted moderators review submissions; approval threshold is configurable.
- Published entries retain provenance and revision history.
- Contributors may be publicly attributed or anonymous on the public site.
- Existing entries can receive proposed revisions.
- Rotuman character input remains an assistive feature, not a search prerequisite.

## Later
Community questions, confirmation signals, richer phrases/grammar resources, and validated pronunciation/audio can grow from the dictionary foundation.

## Governance
The product must be portable by design so stewardship can move from the prototype creator to an organisation such as the NZ Rotuman Fellowship without rebuilding the application or losing contribution history.
